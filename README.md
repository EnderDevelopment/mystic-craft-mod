# Mystic Craft

Mystic Craft is a Minecraft mod designed to enhance your world with magical elements and world-building features. It focuses on simplicity, ease of use, and the addition of various structures to your Minecraft experience. This mod is built using NeoForge and is compatible with Minecraft version 1.20.1.

## Install

1. **Download the mod**: Obtain the latest version of Mystic Craft from the [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/mystic-craft) or [GitHub](https://github.com/EnderDevelopment/MysticCraft).
2. **Install the mod**: Place the downloaded JAR file into the `mods` folder of your Minecraft instance.
3. **Install NeoForge**: Ensure you have NeoForge installed and configured for Minecraft version 1.20.1. You can download NeoForge from the [official website](https://neoforged.net/).

## Use

1. **Launch Minecraft**: Start your Minecraft client with the NeoForge profile.
2. **Explore the world**: Mystic Craft will automatically add magical elements and structures to your world.
3. **Use the Magic Wand**: Find the Magic Wand in your inventory and use it to cast spells and interact with magical elements.
4. **Place Mana Recharge Blocks**: Use Mana Recharge Blocks to recharge your mana and enhance your magical abilities.
